# Code of Conduct

Bot Bureau welcomes contributions from people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Participants are expected to:

- be respectful and constructive;
- assume good intent while discussing technical disagreements;
- focus feedback on the work, not the person;
- protect private data and credentials shared during development; and
- accept responsibility for correcting mistakes.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, deliberate exposure of secrets, and disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to keep the project safe and productive. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's security or maintainer contact channels.
